# The OEIS-open dataset

Upstream source: the `FormalConjectures/OEIS` files hosting at least one
`@[category research open]` statement at the FC commit pinned in `fc_commit`,
vendored verbatim into `Sources/` (flat, upstream basenames).

Unrelated to the `oeis` dataset, which vendors the Tsoukalas-paper
autoformalized OEIS corpus (`FormalConjectures/OEIS/Auto` at an older pin)
with per-conjecture membership; this dataset is simply the research-open
statements of the directory at this pin.

`samples.jsonl` lists every research-open statement in those files (one row
per statement; unscoreable or otherwise problematic members ship as `excluded`
rows with their reason). `research solved` statements sharing the files are
not members. Generated by `scripts/generate_fc_open_isolated.py`.
