# The Wikipedia-autoformalized dataset

Upstream source: the accepted final files of one run of our own autoformalization pipeline (`epoch-research/autoformalization`, Hawk eval set `autoformalize-full-r8zuxgce3jf7ppre`, 2026-09-03; identity and task args in `metadata/run.json`) over Wikipedia's *List of unsolved problems in mathematics* -- every list entry absent from formal-conjectures at the run's pin. Selection, by `scripts/vendor_wikipedia_autoformalized.py`: a sample's final file is vendored verbatim into `Sources/` iff its headline `formalized` score is CORRECT or PARTIAL (the adjudicator accepted a compiling file that survived the settle probe, stating every or some of the decomposed sub-questions) and the adjudicator's confidence that the file faithfully formalizes every slot it states is >= 0.8: 248 of the run's 335 samples. 5 of those import a sibling FC problem module the sandbox images do not carry and are not vendored, leaving 243 files; `metadata/run_samples.jsonl` records every sample's outcome and the reason.

This is our own autoformalization output, not upstream formal-conjectures. `fc_commit` is the FC commit the run compiled against; it plays only its sandbox-image/proving-library role here. The files carry the FC contribution header the pipeline writes; the load-time license-header strip keeps it out of the agent-facing sketch.

`samples.jsonl` lists every research-category statement in those files: the 356 statements the run's adjudicator accepted as faithful formalizations of the decomposed sub-questions (the row's `slot`; 38 of them the formalizers classified research solved -- list entries, or sub-questions of entries, that are known results) plus the formalizers' additional research variants and known-result statements sharing their files. 15 value-typed `answer(sorry)` statements (12 of them kept slots) are excluded rows. Each row's `formalized` and `adjudicator_confidence` are its file's run outcome.

`subsets/adjudicated_open.json` lists the 305 scoreable kept slots classified research open whose statement records no `answer(True/False)` verdict (over 226 problems), `apn_wikipedia_autoformalized`'s default subset. The one research-open kept slot it leaves out for a recorded verdict is the Connes embedding problem, stated `answer(False) ↔ P` after the MIP* = RE negative answer; like every `answer(...) ↔` form the verdict is un-filled in the shipped spec, which states plain `P`.
