# The FC100OpenSet1 dataset

One row per member of the Formal Conjectures paper's frozen 100-problem open
subset (arXiv 2605.13171), as listed in `samples.jsonl` -- the manifest is the
membership source of truth. 86 rows are runnable samples; the 14 value-typed
`answer(sorry)` members are `excluded` rows (the placeholder elaborates to a
position-labeled `sorryAx` *in the statement's type*, so the statement cannot
be closed -- or even stated -- without the paper's `google.answer
"with_auxiliary"` machinery, and SafeVerify cannot score it; the propositional
`answer(sorry) ↔ P` members are instead certified-rewritten to plain `P` at
generation time). Every row's `source` points at the vendored file under
`Sources/` hosting its declaration.

The manifest transcribes the membership list
`FormalConjectures/Subsets/FC100OpenSet1.lean` at the same pin as `Sources/`
(sha256 `82f6f8958c152dfdd2555cedcee83887b94b7f5520c8cb171a7dd522ea3795c1`);
the file itself is not vendored -- it is recoverable from the pinned public
commit, and the transcription was verified against a byte-verbatim copy at
conversion time. The paper's `bench-v1-lean4.27.0` tag is an ancestor of the
pin; the subset file is byte-identical at both, and of its 88 problem files
exactly three differ: doc-comment-only edits in
`Arxiv/0912.2382/CurlingNumberConjecture.lean` and
`Arxiv/2501.03234/ArithmeticSumS.lean`, and
`Books/UniformDistributionOfSequences/Equidistribution.lean`, whose two
`def`s (`IsEquidistributed`, `IsEquidistributedModuloOne`) moved verbatim
into `FormalConjecturesForMathlib`, which the sandbox images already
contain. Results are comparable to the tag.

`Isolated/` (one per-target spec per kept member, with sibling theorems and
`example` commands cut, propositional `answer(sorry) ↔ P` statements
certified-rewritten to plain `P`, and FC's `@[category ..., AMS ...]`
classification lists dropped -- catalogue metadata whose category field
records resolution status) is generated by
`scripts/generate_fc100_isolated.py` and validated by
`tests/test_fc100_isolation.py`.

Third-party material: `Sources/` (vendored Formal Conjectures files,
Apache-2.0) carries a README with its exact provenance.
